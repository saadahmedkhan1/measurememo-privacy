Privacy Policy for MeasureMemo

Effective date: September 10, 2026

MeasureMemo is developed and maintained by Saad Ahmed Khan. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the MeasureMemo Android application.

Information collection

MeasureMemo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud-storage services.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter into MeasureMemo, including card titles, dimensions, product details, notes, and other saved specifications, is stored locally on your device.

Photos that you select or take for your cards are also processed and stored locally for use within the app. This information is not uploaded to the developer or to an external server.

Camera and photo access

MeasureMemo may request access to your device camera when you choose to take a photo. When you select an existing photo, the app uses Android's system photo-selection interface.

Camera and photo access is used only to attach an image to a MeasureMemo card. MeasureMemo does not access these features without an action initiated by you.

Smart Paste

The Smart Paste feature analyzes text that you manually paste into the app to suggest information such as dimensions and product details. This processing takes place locally on your device. The pasted text is not sent to the developer or to an external service.

Data sharing

MeasureMemo does not share user information with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Your saved information remains on your device until you delete the relevant cards, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Because the developer does not receive or store your information, there is no remote user data or account to delete.

Children's privacy

MeasureMemo is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed toward children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

MeasureMemo is designed to keep its information locally on your device. The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if MeasureMemo's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page, together with a revised effective date.
